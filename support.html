Nare Support

Questions, bug reports or a mistake in a card: email bilguuniqo@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version and, for a card, the word and its deck.

Adding the widgets

Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, tap Lock Screen, tap the widget area, then add Nare Kanji and Nare Sentence. Home Screen: touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, tap Add Widget, then choose Nare.

The large card isn't on my Lock Screen

Open Nare to bring it back. iOS ends a Live Activity after about 12 hours, and when the app is force-quit or updated. Check that Live Activities are on in Settings, under Nare.

Your data

Your cards and progress stay on your iPhone, and deleting the app removes them. See the privacy policy.
