Nare Support

Questions, bug reports or a mistake in a card: email bilguuniqo@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version, the app version and, for a card, the word and its deck. In the app, Settings > Help > Email support fills these in for you, and Report a mistake on any card does the same for that card.

Adding the widgets

Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, tap Lock Screen, tap the widget area, then add Nare Kanji and Nare Sentence. The same spot also has a round and a one-line Nare widget that show the current word. Home Screen: touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, tap Add Widget, then choose Nare. The app walks you through this in Settings > Lock Screen setup.

Adding your own deck

Open the Decks tab, tap +, then Import deck. Choose a file, paste text, or enter a web address, check the preview, then tap Add at the top right. To add cards one at a time, tap +, then New empty deck.

Use a CSV or TSV file with one card per line. Each line needs a term and a meaning:

猫,cat
犬,dog

A meaning with a comma in it goes in double quotes. A first row can name more columns in any order: term, meaning, reading, example, note, translation, phrase and parts. Without that row, the preview lets you say what each column holds. Tap What format? on the import screen for the full guide and a sample file. Anki packages (.apkg) can't be read: from Anki on a computer, choose File, then Export, and set the format to Notes in Plain Text.

Adding cards to a deck you already have

Open the deck, tap +, then tap Import cards. New cards are added. Cards you already have keep their progress and take the new text, unless you edited them yourself. Nothing is removed.

To keep a deck updated, leave Keep updating on when you import a file or web address. Nare then checks each time you open the app. A web address has to work without a sign-in, so anyone with the link can read it. For a private deck, keep the file in iCloud Drive.

The large card isn't on my Lock Screen

Open Nare to bring it back, or add the Nare button to Control Center (iOS 18 or later). iOS ends a Live Activity after about 12 hours, and when the app is force-quit or updated. A daily reminder is in Settings. Check that Live Activities are on in iOS Settings, under Nare.

Studying more than one deck

Tick any decks on the Decks tab, or tap the deck name at the top of Review. Each keeps its own progress and daily limits, and their cards are mixed together. To stop, untick a deck: its progress is kept.

A word disappeared

After 8 misses a word is paused, and Nare tells you when it happens. Find it in its deck and tap Resume. You can change the number in Settings > Advanced.

Why only 10 new words a day?

More new words now means more reviews in the coming days. Change it in Settings, or tap Learn 5 more when you finish for the day. Limits count per deck. When reviews are held back by the limit, new words wait too, so a backlog can't keep growing.

Why does it ask for Face ID?

iOS can ask for Face ID or your passcode before a Lock Screen button runs while the phone is locked.

Moving to a new iPhone

In Settings, open Your data and export a backup to Files. On the new iPhone, install Nare and restore that file.

Your data

Your cards and progress stay on your iPhone, and deleting the app removes them. Export a backup first from Settings > Your data. See the privacy policy.
