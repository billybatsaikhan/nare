Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

What this app collects

Nothing. The app has no account, ads, analytics or tracking, and includes no third-party code. We receive none of your data.

What stays on your iPhone

Your cards, review history and deck choices are stored only on your device, so the app and its widgets can use them. They aren't sent to us.

Importing from a URL

If you choose to import a deck from a web address, your iPhone connects directly to the server you entered. That server's owner may see the request, as with any web request, and their own privacy policy applies. Nothing is sent to us.

Third parties

We don't share data with anyone, because we don't have any. Apple may provide us with general App Store statistics and crash reports, subject to your device settings and Apple's privacy policy.

Keeping or deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. To remove it all, delete the app. iPhone backups you've made may keep a copy until those backups are replaced.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page. Questions: bilguuniqo@gmail.com
